Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous modeling experience?

No. Our courses are suitable for beginners as well as individuals who already have modeling experience.

What age do I need to be?

Our standard courses are available to students aged 16 and above.

What will I learn?

Students can learn runway walking, posing, photography, personal presentation, confidence and professional modeling skills.

How do I register?

You can register by completing the online registration form on our Registration page.

Where is the academy located?

The Modeling Academy is based in Cape Town, South Africa.

Do you offer workshops and events?

Yes. The academy hosts workshops, fashion shows and other activities throughout the year.
